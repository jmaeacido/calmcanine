Privacy policy

How we collect, use, and care for your information when you shop with Calm Canine.

Overview

Calm Canine (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you browse our site or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details — if you create an account, we store your email, a hashed password, and any contact or shipping details you save. You can still place an order as a guest without an account.
  • Order details — name, email, phone, shipping address, items purchased, amounts, and order ID. Guest and account orders both require this information to fulfill a purchase.
  • Payment details — we do not store full card numbers on our servers. In the current checkout stub, only card brand and last four digits may be saved with your order. When a live payment provider is connected, card data is handled by that provider under their security standards.
  • Newsletter — if you opt in, we store your email (and name if provided) for occasional product updates. The prompt is skipped after you subscribe.
  • Cart data — your cart may be stored in your browser (local storage) so items remain when you return. We also remember a newsletter opt-in in local storage so we do not ask again after you subscribe.
  • Basic technical data — standard server or browser information needed to run the site (such as approximate device/browser type). We do not sell personal information.

How we use information

  • To process, fulfill, and support your orders
  • To send optional newsletter updates if you subscribe
  • To prevent fraud, enforce our policies, and meet legal obligations (including age and CBD shipping rules)
  • To improve our website and customer experience

Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the shop — for example payment processing, email delivery, shipping carriers, and hosting — only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or safety.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & local storage

We use essential browser storage (such as your cart) so checkout works, and a session cookie if you sign in. You can clear site data in your browser settings; doing so may empty your cart and sign you out.

Data retention

We keep order records as long as needed for fulfillment, accounting, customer support, and legal compliance. You may request access to or correction of your order contact details by contacting us with your order ID.

Children

Calm Canine is intended for adults. You must be 21 or older to purchase. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Update saved account details from your account, or update shipping or contact details by reaching out before an order ships when possible.
  • Ask questions about the data tied to a specific order ID.
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing emails once marketing email is enabled (transactional order emails may still be sent).

Changes

We may update this policy as the shop grows (for example when live payments or email providers are connected). The “Last updated” date below will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us using the email on your order confirmation or the contact method listed on our site, and include your order ID when relevant.
