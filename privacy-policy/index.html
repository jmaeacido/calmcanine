Introduction

This privacy policy (“Policy”) describes how Native Ceuticals in North Carolina (“Company,” “we,” and “our”) processes, collects, uses and shares personal data when using this website, https://nativeceutical.com (the “Website”). Please read this information carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal data.

Purposes of Processing

Personal data means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. We process personal data to operate the Website, provide products and services, communicate with you, fulfil orders, improve our business, comply with legal obligations and protect our legitimate interests. Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw it at any time.

Collecting Your Personal Data

We collect information you give us when you register, upload content, participate in rewards or promotions, request support, make purchases, complete forms or correspond with us. If you connect through a social networking service, we may receive information made available by that service.

We may also automatically collect device, browser and usage information, IP address, pages viewed, links selected, referring pages and cookie identifiers for analytics, security, personalization and the provision of our services.

Cookies

Cookies are small data files placed on your device. Essential cookies enable core functions; functionality cookies remember choices; analytics and performance cookies help us understand use; and advertising or social-media cookies may provide relevant content and measure campaigns.

We may use Google Analytics. Learn about Google’s privacy practices or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You may disable cookies in your browser, although some Website functions may not work correctly.

Advertising

We may work with advertising partners that use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements based on your interactions with the Website and other services.

Using Your Personal Data

We may use personal data to operate and improve the Website; administer accounts, rewards and promotions; provide support; send service notices and permitted marketing; process payments and purchases; comply with law; prevent fraud; analyze use; and share information as described in this Policy. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Sharing Your Personal Data

We may share information with designated third parties, vendors and service providers that help us operate, process payments, fulfill orders, host the Website, provide analytics or deliver communications. Third-party sites linked from the Website have their own privacy policies.

User Generated Content

Information you post publicly may be viewed, collected and used by others. Please use care when disclosing personal information.

International Data Transfer

Your data may be processed outside your place of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data. No Internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Retention

We retain personal data as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy and to meet legal, accounting and reporting requirements.

Our Policy on Children

The Website is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to opt out, access or amend your data, request a portable copy, or request erasure. We may need to verify your identity.

Complaints

If you have a concern about our privacy practices, contact us. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Contact Information

For questions or requests concerning this Policy, email Info@nativeceuticals.com.
