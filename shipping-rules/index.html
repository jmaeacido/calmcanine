Shipping rules

How we ship Calm Canine treats across the United States.

Shipping Refunds Privacy CBD restrictions

Where we ship

We currently ship within the contiguous United States only. We do not ship to P.O. boxes, APO/FPO addresses, Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, or international destinations at this time.

Some states restrict the sale or shipment of CBD pet products. See our CBD Sales Restrictions for the full list. Checkout will block restricted states automatically.

Shipping rates

  • $5.99 flat-rate shipping on orders under $35.
  • Free shipping on orders of $35 or more (before tax).

Rates are calculated at checkout based on your cart subtotal. Tax is estimated separately by shipping state.

Processing & delivery

  • Orders are typically prepared within 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed.
  • Once shipped, most deliveries arrive within 3–7 business days, depending on carrier and destination.
  • You will receive order updates by email when mail delivery is connected to your account email.

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Weather, carrier delays, and remote locations can add time.

Subscriptions

Subscribe & Save orders ship on the schedule you choose (starting with a 1-month plan). Shipping rules and free-shipping thresholds apply to each shipment the same way they apply to one-time orders.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your shipping name, street address, city, state, and ZIP before placing your order. We are not responsible for packages delivered to an incorrect address you provided. If a package is returned to us as undeliverable, contact us to arrange reshipment; additional shipping charges may apply.

Lost or damaged packages

If your order never arrives or arrives damaged, reach out with your order ID and photos (for damage). We will work with the carrier and, when appropriate, replace the order or issue a refund under our Refund Policy.
