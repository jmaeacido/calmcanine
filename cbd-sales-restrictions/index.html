Age requirement

You must be 21 years of age or older to purchase Calm Canine. By placing an order, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

States we cannot ship to

Because state rules for CBD pet products vary, we do not ship Calm Canine to the following states:

  • Alabama (AL)
  • Idaho (ID)
  • Kansas (KS)
  • Mississippi (MS)
  • Nebraska (NE)
  • North Dakota (ND)
  • South Carolina (SC)
  • Wyoming (WY)

If you select a restricted state at checkout, your order cannot be completed. This list may change as laws change; checkout is the source of truth for what we can ship today.

About our product

  • Calm Canine treats use CBD isolate.
  • Each square is formulated with 10mg CBD isolate and 0mg Δ9 THC as stated on our product materials.
  • This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
  • Always consult your veterinarian before introducing CBD or any new supplement, especially if your dog is pregnant, nursing, on medication, or has a health condition.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for knowing and following the laws that apply where you live and where the product will be used. Do not order for shipment into a restricted state or for anyone under 21.
